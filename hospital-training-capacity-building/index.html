WHY TRAINING FAILS

Most Hospital Training Does Not Change Performance

Many hospitals conduct induction programmes, occasional lectures, or accreditation-related training.

Yet the same operational problems continue. Why? Because training is often:

  • generic
  • theoretical
  • disconnected from daily work
  • not role-specific
  • not measured
  • not reinforced
  • not linked to KPIs
  • not followed by supervision
Training happens. Behaviour does not change.

At VR Care, training is designed around the actual gaps observed in the hospital.

OUR APPROACH

Training Based on Real Hospital Problems

Before designing a programme, we first understand:

  • What is going wrong?
  • Which departments are affected?
  • Is the problem knowledge, attitude, process, supervision or staffing?
  • Are SOPs available?
  • Are KPIs defined?
  • Do employees understand what is expected from them?
  • Is the issue individual or systemic?
  • Is management measuring the right indicators?

This allows us to create training that is specific, measurable and relevant.

Our training model combines:

Assessment→Training→Practice→Monitoring→Feedback→Improvement

This makes capacity building a continuous management process rather than a one-day event.

SOP-BASED TRAINING

An SOP Is Useful Only When Staff Can Follow It

Many hospitals have excellent SOP manuals that employees rarely read.

VR Care converts SOPs into operational training. Instead of simply handing over a document, we help teams understand:

  • what must be done
  • who must do it
  • when it must be done
  • what must be documented
  • when escalation is required
  • what KPI will measure compliance

This makes SOP implementation much more practical.

TRAINING METHODOLOGY

How We Build Capability

  1. Gap AssessmentObserve performance, interview teams and review KPIs.
  2. Training Need AnalysisIdentify what employees need to know, change or improve.
  3. Custom Programme DesignCreate content according to role, department and hospital priorities.
  4. Practical TrainingUse hospital-specific examples, case studies, simulations and real scenarios.
  5. AssessmentCheck understanding through practical exercises, observation or short evaluations.
  6. Post-Training MonitoringReview whether behaviour and performance actually improve.
  7. Refresher & CoachingReinforce key skills where required.
