ABOUT

Who is Afzal Shaikh?

Afzal Shaikh is a hospital operations and management consultant with over 25 years of experience in hospital administration and operations. He works with hospital owners and leadership teams through VR Care and is the founder of Super Dr.

His work brings together departmental coordination, financial visibility, patient experience, workforce development and technology. The focus is practical: helping hospital teams translate plans into daily routines, clear responsibilities and measurable progress.

WHAT HE DOES

Helping hospitals work better, every day.

Hospital operations & management

Department coordination, OPD and IPD workflows, ICU and OT readiness, discharge processes, escalation systems and owner reporting.

Hospital audit & turnaround

Identify operational gaps, review utilisation and costs, prioritise corrective action and support implementation with defined responsibilities.

Billing, TPA & financial control

Strengthen billing discipline, collections visibility, insurance coordination, receivables follow-up and departmental performance reviews.

Leadership & team development

Support CEO and administrator selection, HOD development, staffing structures, KRAs, KPIs and training that connects accountability with daily work.

Patient inflow & service development

Improve enquiry handling, appointment follow-up, consultant coordination, service visibility and patient relationships through structured CRM processes.

Hospital projects & digital workflows

Operational inputs for hospital planning, SOPs, accreditation readiness and appropriate use of HMIS, dashboards and AI-assisted administrative workflows.

SUPER DR™

Founder, Super Dr™

Years of working within hospitals inform Afzal’s approach to healthcare software: systems should make work easier for doctors, nurses, administrators and patients.

Super Dr brings together hospital information systems and connected workflows. His consulting work examines how technology is actually used, from registration and billing to clinical coordination, reporting and patient follow-up.

CRM and AI-assisted workflows can support appointments, reminders and routine enquiries where they fit the hospital’s needs.

APPROACH

Clarity for owners. Support for teams.

Effective management begins with understanding the people delivering care and the constraints they face.

Afzal’s approach combines listening to teams, checking source data, setting practical priorities and reviewing progress with leadership. The aim is to build processes that staff can follow and owners can assess.

He also shares hospital operations insights on LinkedIn for hospital owners, administrators and healthcare professionals.

HOW WE WORK

From the first discussion to implementation.

  1. Understand your hospital.Discuss its size, specialties, current performance and the issues that need attention.
  2. Establish the baseline.Review available data, workflows, staffing and departmental responsibilities.
  3. Agree the engagement.Define the scope, team, onsite involvement, authority, reporting, commercials and priorities.
